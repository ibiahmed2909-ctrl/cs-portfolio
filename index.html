WHO I AM

I'm Ibrahim, a second-year BSc Computer Science student at Aston University. I've always been curious about how computers, software and apps actually work behind the screen, and that curiosity is what pointed me towards Computer Science. I like solving problems and I like being creative, and programming lets me do both at the same time.

This year I'm studying Data Structures and Object-Oriented Programming, Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, and Business and Enterprise. The first two are changing how I plan and structure my code, and the AI module is making me think about problems in a completely different way. Business and Enterprise is a good reminder that software exists to solve real problems for real people, not just to run.

What I enjoy most is turning an idea into something that actually works. Web development and interactive applications are where I'm happiest, especially the interface side, where small details like spacing, colour and motion decide whether something feels professional. Seeing something I built from scratch finally run never really gets old.

I'm now looking for a year-in-industry placement or internship where I can learn from experienced people and contribute properly, ideally in cybersecurity, software engineering or data analysis.
